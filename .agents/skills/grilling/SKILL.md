---
name: grilling
description: "Stress-test a plan or decision when the user explicitly asks to be grilled."
---

Find the assumptions most likely to invalidate the plan. Check available facts
yourself; use one agent unless delegation is requested. Reuse answers already given.

Ask a small round of high-value questions, preferably one to three, with your
recommendation and its tradeoff. Wait for answers before asking dependent questions.
Focus on scope, evidence, feasibility, and decisions that are costly to reverse.
Do not enumerate every hypothetical branch.

Stop when material uncertainties are resolved or the user asks to proceed.
Summarize decisions and remaining assumptions. Grilling alone does not authorize
implementation; an existing instruction to implement still applies.
